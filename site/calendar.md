## Add it to your own calendar

To see the sessions in Outlook or Google Calendar, subscribe to this address:

```text
https://calendar.zoho.eu/ical/0801124a41d4b027f47596a5be452b810000721a936b9712877ce0ca036b0686252edef5522343812561137d87291c2203a9821077683ff085692955e81f602ea37135d5775ebf6afa2166d60a35
```

- **Outlook:** in the calendar, **Add calendar** -> **Subscribe from web** (in the
  classic Outlook: **Add Calendar** -> **From Internet**), then paste the address.
- **Google Calendar:** **Other calendars** -> **+** -> **From URL**, then paste the
  address.

The changes then come to your calendar by themselves, but not at once: it can take
up to a day. If your work Outlook does not allow such calendars, use this page.
